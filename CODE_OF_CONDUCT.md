# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive in this project's issues, pull requests,
reviews, and other community spaces.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized attention,
publishing another person's private information, and sustained personal attacks are
not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome; hostility toward the people in
the discussion is not.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report
conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact options. Do
not use a public issue when doing so would expose a target or sensitive information.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repeated behavior, and the
safety of those involved. Good-faith correction and repair are encouraged.
